How to read these bars — methodology & caveats
  • Denominator is described, not estimated. For clades where described << true diversity (Insects, Fungi, Nematodes, most microbial groups), the displayed coverage overstates the share of real biodiversity sampled.
  • Datasets, not a clean partition. Each bar is one published dataset, so some clades (e.g. Birds, Fish) appear more than once. The 61-row partition view used in Figure 1 of the paper rolls duplicates up and adds “Other…” catch-alls to cover the long tail.
  • Prokaryotes are not comparable. For Bacteria and Archaea the denominator is GTDB genome clusters, not Linnaean species — the bar is shown on the same axis but should not be read against eukaryote bars.
  • Taxonomic imputation. Four large trees include imputed tips, not molecular data: seed plants 22%, fish 37%, squamates 43%, sharks 51%. Excluding imputed tips drops overall eukaryote coverage from ~23% to ~9%.
